Fees est. & APR est. are estimates from local snapshots stored in this browser since your first visit to each pool. Fee growth is measured from how much each LP token has grown in pool value — swap fees raise it, while adding or removing liquidity does not. APR annualises that growth and needs a window of at least 1 hour. The mainnet version will use a real indexer for full history.

Deploy the entire DEX from your own wallet, with every signature through your wallet. The steps run in order and your progress is saved in this browser. You need testnet HYPE for gas: claim it from the official faucet or QuickNode.

1

Wrapped HYPE (WHYPE)

Not done

The Router needs a wrapped version of HYPE. The canonical WHYPE from the official Elysium docs is the best choice: your pools line up with the token everyone else uses.

2

UniswapV2Factory

Not done

The trading pair factory. feeToSetter is set to your wallet address.

3

UniswapV2Router02

Not done

The contract used by the Swap and Liquidity tabs. Built from the Factory + WHYPE from step 1.

Already have a Factory (for example from an earlier deploy)? Paste its address here and this step deploys a replacement Router against that factory with canonical WHYPE, instead of requiring a fresh Factory deploy. Leave it empty to use the Factory from step 2.

4

TestToken (tUSD)

Not done

A freely mintable test ERC20 ("Elysium Test USD"), for trading and seeding your first pool.

5

BondingLaunchpad

Not done

The pump.fun-style memecoin launchpad behind the Launchpad tab. Built from the Router from step 3; its protocol fees are sent to your wallet.

6

PresaleFactory

Not done

The presale / IDO factory behind the IDO tab. Built from the Router from step 3; its protocol fees are sent to your wallet.

Create your own token

A plain ERC-20 with your own name and symbol. The full amount is minted to your wallet at creation: no owner, no further minting, no taxes. Use it as the sale token in the IDO tab, or trade and pool it like any other token.
