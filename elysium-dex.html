Create a presale

Run a fixed-price token sale in HYPE. If the soft cap is reached, the raise pairs with your liquidity tokens into a Ladder pool at the listing rate; if not, contributors get refunded.

After creating, you must approve the presale contract for the sale + liquidity tokens and press Deposit on the presale card below, or the sale cannot start collecting.

Presales

Deploy the entire DEX from your own wallet, with every signature through MetaMask. The steps run in order and your progress is saved in this browser. You need testnet HYPE for gas: claim it from the official faucet or QuickNode.

1

Wrapped HYPE (WHYPE)

Not done

The Router needs a wrapped version of HYPE. The canonical WHYPE from the official Elysium docs is the best choice: your pools line up with the token everyone else uses.

2

UniswapV2Factory

Not done

The trading pair factory. feeToSetter is set to your wallet address.

3

UniswapV2Router02

Not done

The contract used by the Swap and Liquidity tabs. Built from the Factory + WHYPE from step 1.

Already have a Factory (for example from an earlier deploy)? Paste its address here and this step deploys a replacement Router against that factory with canonical WHYPE, instead of requiring a fresh Factory deploy. Leave it empty to use the Factory from step 2.

4

TestToken (tUSD)

Not done

A freely mintable test ERC20 ("Elysium Test USD"), for trading and seeding your first pool.

5

BondingLaunchpad

Not done

The pump.fun-style memecoin launchpad behind the Launchpad tab. Built from the Router from step 3; its protocol fees are sent to your wallet.

6

PresaleFactory

Not done

The presale / IDO factory behind the IDO tab. Built from the Router from step 3; its protocol fees are sent to your wallet.
